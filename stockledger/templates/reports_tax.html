Before you use these figures

  • This is a bookkeeping summary, not tax advice. Check it against HMRC guidance or with an accountant before filing.
  • If your turnover was under £90,000 you can normally use the short self-employment pages (SA103S) and enter total expenses as a single figure; otherwise use the full pages (SA103F).
  • If your total self-employment income was £1,000 or less you may be able to use the £1,000 trading allowance instead of claiming expenses. You can't do both.
  • Making Tax Digital for Income Tax is already compulsory for sole traders with qualifying income over £50,000 (from April 2026), dropping to £30,000 from April 2027 and £20,000 from April 2028. If that applies to you, you need HMRC-recognised software to send quarterly updates - this app prepares the figures but cannot submit to HMRC.
  • Not included: mileage allowances, use of home, capital allowances on cars, personal drawings (not an expense) and any private-use adjustments.
